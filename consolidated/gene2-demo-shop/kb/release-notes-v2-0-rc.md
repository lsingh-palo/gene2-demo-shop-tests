Release candidate 1, deployed to Staging on 22 September 2026. Target release: 9 October
2026.
v2.0 moves the shop to the new front-end platform. Customer-facing behaviour is
unchanged unless listed below; QA confirms this with the v1 vs v2 parity run.
New
All products: 18 products, search, category and in-stock filters, 8 per page.
Product pages with ratings and customer reviews.
Stock levels: "Out of stock" and "Only N left".
Promo codes SAVE10, WELCOME5 and FREESHIP; one discount code per order.
Delivery regions: Metro, Regional and Remote.
Order history.
Admin: stock levels and promo code switches.
Changed
Tax is charged on the subtotal after discounts.
The free-shipping threshold is measured after discounts.
Known issues in RC1
Two discount codes can be applied to one order.
Regional deliveries are charged the Metro fee.
Carried over from v1.0: missing postcode accepted; double submit records two orders.
